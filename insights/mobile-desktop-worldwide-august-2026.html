Mobile edges ahead of desktop in August's global page-view figures

StatCounter's August 2026 figures put mobile at 49.36% and desktop at 49.11%. The important message is not to neglect either experience.

Event date: 31 August 2026

49.36%
Written and published 10 November 2026. The date above refers to the event or measurement period, not the original publication of this article.

A very close result

For August 2026, StatCounter recorded 49.36% of tracked worldwide page views on mobile devices, 49.11% on desktop and 1.54% on tablets, allowing for rounding. The date here marks the end of the measured month, not an asserted publication date for the dataset.

Mobile came out just ahead in this snapshot, but the difference was slim. One month's figures do not establish a permanent trend, and global traffic shares can be very different from an individual business's audience.

What it means for design

The mistake would be to treat mobile as the simplified version of a desktop website. On a small screen, customers still need clear information, fast pages, readable text and straightforward ways to enquire, reserve or purchase.

At the same time, desktop remains crucial for many research-heavy and business-to-business journeys. An effective website should support both environments instead of obsessing over a single device category.

Use your own evidence

Global figures make a useful reminder, but analytics from your own website are a better guide to how your customers behave. Look at device mix alongside enquiry rates, page engagement and important user journeys.

Mobile-first design should mean prioritising the essentials and making the experience genuinely usable, not discarding the desktop audience.

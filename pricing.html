A few useful answers.

Still not sure which package fits? Tell us what you need and we'll point you towards the right option.

Do I have to pay for hosting?

Hosting and care are charged separately from launch. Our Hosted plan starts at £29/month. If you'd rather arrange your own hosting, we can discuss a handover and what technical support would remain available.

What do the 30 days of post-launch support include?

We'll fix defects in the website we've delivered if it isn't working as agreed, at no extra charge during the first 30 days. New features, design changes and additional content are separate. This doesn't affect any legal rights you may have.

Who writes my website content?

We do. You tell us about your business, services and preferences; we write and arrange the website copy for your approval. Professional photography or extensive specialist content may be quoted separately.

What does Complete include?

Routine changes plus occasional larger updates and new content that fit your existing website. It's a care service, not an unlimited rebuild or marketing contract. We'll agree the scope of substantial requests before starting; major development and ongoing SEO are quoted separately. There's no rollover allowance or obligation to request work each month.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once your project has been paid for, you own the completed website and its agreed content, subject to any third-party licences. We can arrange handover if you choose another provider.

Can I cancel a care plan?

Yes. Plans renew monthly and you can cancel with one month's notice. We'll help explain the options for keeping your website online elsewhere if you leave.

Are there other costs?

Domain renewals, third-party subscriptions and professional photography are separate where required. We'll explain those before you commit. Allerton Digital is not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged.
